# Governance: System Architect Proof of Work

**Date:** 2026-11-21  
**Repository:** SystemArchitectDGCP/SystemArchitect-Proof-of-Work  
**Classification:** Public repository governance  
**Authority:** System Architect DGCP™  
**License:** DGCP | MMFARM-POL-2025

## 1. Mandate and boundary

Maintain a traceable **public presentation index** of authorized work by the DGCP™ System Architect. This repository is not the authoritative archive of original MaMeeFarm™ Real-Work Data, daily Data Units, Proofs, timestamps, ledgers, or confidential organizational operations.

Public visibility of a repository is **not** a grant of blanket permission to distribute the upstream records it references.

## 2. Continuity of existing work

The ongoing daily work, its data capture, evidence generation, recording, commit sequence and corrections **remain in the original repositories and approved environments**. In particular:

- [MaMeeFarm-Data](https://github.com/MaMeeFarm-Data) remains a separate source account.
- [MaMeeFarm-DGCP](https://github.com/MaMeeFarm-Data/MaMeeFarm-DGCP) retains its historical protocol and governance documentation.
- [DGCP-Specs](https://github.com/MaMeeFarm-Data/DGCP-Specs) retains its published specifications in their original version contexts.
- [SystemArchitectDGCP](https://github.com/SystemArchitectDGCP) retains the separate architecture and institutional workspace.

No data migration, mirroring, resequencing, renaming, license transfer or retrospective rewriting is authorized by the establishment of this new repository.

## 3. Record acceptance

An entry in `work/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md` is eligible only when:

1. The underlying output and its source are identified precisely.
2. The output is **approved for public disclosure**.
3. The stated outcome is supported by an accessible, appropriately scoped public record or by another specifically authorized public citation.
4. The entry distinguishes work completion, public publication and independent verification.
5. The entry includes its date, public URL, applicable limitations, correction references when needed, and the license reference.

If any of these are absent, the work remains in its existing source location. **Do not create a placeholder, synthetic work event or misleading success claim to fill a calendar date.**

## 4. Publication and authorization

The System Architect retains authority to approve content, rights-sensitive releases and publication. Assistants or automation may prepare drafts, index already approved public outputs and conduct supporting checks, but they must not independently represent draft work as approved or release restricted source material.

**Public allowed:** approved descriptions, public websites, published documentation and externally verifiable milestones.

**Not automatically allowed:** Raw Data, Proof and DataUnit contents, OTS, IPFS CIDs, daily ledgers, operational identifiers, credentials, private GitHub materials, protected research, proprietary methods, detailed security architecture or inferred internal workflows.

A public work entry is a summary **about** the work, not an unreviewed copy of underlying evidence.

## 5. Attribution and evidence boundaries

- Attribute human work to its human originator, and label assistant-produced drafts or automated transformations correctly.
- A GitHub commit verifies repository history within its technical scope; a contribution graph summarizes activity. Neither by itself proves the truth of an originating real-world event.
- A digest, CID or timestamp may substantiate defined technical properties but does not independently establish factual truth, legal compliance, certification or third-party audit.
- Do not combine protected source fragments to reveal a private operational method indirectly.
- Respect the applicable governing instruments of each linked source; do not assume one repository's visibility or license overrides another's.

## 6. Append-only correction and lifecycle

- Preserve the identity, sequence and previous record state of work at its original source.
- Add new daily public-facing entries only for approved outputs; older entries remain independently attributable.
- When a material error is discovered, add a dated correction record that identifies the affected entry and states its status; maintain an explicit supersession link. Do not silently rewrite the historical claim.
- Administrative corrections to navigation or formatting may be made with a traceable Git commit, without altering the meaning of historical evidence.
- Never backdate a record to make it appear contemporaneous with work that was only later documented or approved.

## 7. Daily cadence

**Daily production continues as before in the original working repositories.** This repository does not impose a second daily commit requirement. Public Proof-of-Work entries may be published incrementally when authorized, even if original work was completed earlier.

Each public entry must use:

```text
work/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md
```

and include the mandatory footer:

```text
License: DGCP | MMFARM-POL-2025
```

Dates must indicate whether they refer to the original work event, report drafting or public release. No new Asset ID, Proof ID or DataUnit ID may be inferred from this presentation record.

## 8. Rights and privacy

The repository is rights-reserved under the applicable DGCP | MMFARM-POL-2025 license reference. The separate [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) is a rights notice and must not be interpreted as a newly invented license instrument.

If any restricted information is accidentally exposed, stop redistribution and follow the source owner's authorized correction and incident-handling process; a follow-up commit alone is not a guarantee that third-party copies disappear.

## 9. Governance changes

A change to this policy must include the decision date, affected scope, reason, authority and effective status, with a traceable commit. Do not retroactively alter source-governance rules of the original repositories.
