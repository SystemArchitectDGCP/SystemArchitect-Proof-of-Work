## Authority

The System Architect determines what may be disclosed from work under the Architect's authority. No assistant, automation, third party, repository visitor or contributor may independently grant system-use rights or approve disclosure of private material.

## Purpose and Limits

This repository identifies the System Architect and communicates the DGCP™ framework's ownership and commercial-access position. Its name does **not** obligate the owner to create daily entries, supply proof files, expose milestones, publish internal work histories, or operate a public evidence-verification service.

**There is no public reporting cadence, mandatory record format or proof-disclosure commitment.** The earlier draft descriptions of work logs, work dates, and public acceptance workflows are withdrawn as repository requirements by this owner-directed clarification.

## Framework Use

The DGCP™ framework is **not made available for free use**. Access to and use of the framework/system requires express owner authorization and an applicable **paid license or commercial agreement**. Viewing the repository is not a license to deploy, implement, copy, modify, package, commercialize, or sublicense the system.

This is a repository-level policy and does not automatically override valid grants under other authoritative licenses or contracts.

## Confidentiality and Original Records

Original Proof, DU, Raw Data, ledgers, OTS, CIDs, protected architecture, methods, unpublished research, internal operational information, and private repository contents are outside this repository's disclosure scope. The owner retains control of original records.

The distinct GitHub accounts [MaMeeFarm-Data](https://github.com/MaMeeFarm-Data) and [SystemArchitectDGCP](https://github.com/SystemArchitectDGCP) remain separate. A link or technical access does not constitute authorization to inspect, copy or publish restricted content.

No migration, mirroring or rewriting of historical source repositories is authorized.

## Changes and Traceability

Changes to published repository statements must follow owner instruction and be traceable through Git commits. Prior wording remains in Git history; the current approved repository position is this file together with the [Commercial Access Policy](FRAMEWORK-OWNERSHIP-AND-COMMERCIAL-ACCESS-POLICY.md).

Assistant-produced content must not be represented as owner-authored evidence of a real-world event, a certification, an audit or a new legal instrument.
