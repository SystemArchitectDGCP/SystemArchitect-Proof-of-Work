## Eligible records

A milestone record may identify a completed public website release, an approved publication or another observable output when its source, scope, date, limitations and disclosure authorization are established.

Do not equate a GitHub contribution count, commit, published file, cryptographic check or self-declaration with independent certification of the underlying real-world event.

Each record must distinguish the work date, release date and what has actually been verified. An unapproved or restricted milestone remains in its original source system.

## Source integrity

Existing MaMeeFarm™ and DGCP™ records are not migrated, mirrored, resequenced or modified. Corrections must be traceable forward.
